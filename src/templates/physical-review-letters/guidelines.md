# Physical Review Letters authoring contract

The manuscript uses the official APS REVTeX 4.2 class with the `prl` journal
option. APS identifies REVTeX as the recommended authoring system and limits a
Letter's core text to 3750 words, with up to two pages of end matter. See the
[APS author information](https://journals.aps.org/prl/authors) and the
[REVTeX home page](https://journals.aps.org/revtex) before submission.
