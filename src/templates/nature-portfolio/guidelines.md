# Nature Portfolio Authoring Scaffold

This is a project-authored preparation scaffold, not an official Nature
Portfolio LaTeX template. Select the target journal before drafting and follow
its current author instructions for article type, limits, figures, reporting
checklists, editorial policies, and supplementary material.

Nature's initial-submission guidance is at
https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/initial-submission . Nature Portfolio
states that journal-specific requirements apply at
https://www.nature.com/nature-portfolio/for-authors/write .

Keep the standard manuscript sections, data availability, code availability,
acknowledgements, author contributions, and competing interests current and
accurate. Do not describe this scaffold as an official Nature template.
